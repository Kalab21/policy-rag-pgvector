# Data Privacy and Retention Policy

## Retention periods
Loan application records are kept for 7 years after the loan is closed or the application is declined. Call recordings are kept for 2 years. Marketing preferences are kept until the customer withdraws consent.

## Access to personal data
Access to customer personal data is limited to staff whose role requires it. Access to Social Security numbers and bank account numbers requires an additional approval and is logged. Staff may not copy customer data to personal devices.

## Data subject requests
A customer may request a copy of their personal data or ask for it to be corrected. The company responds within 30 days. Requests are verified against the identity on file before any data is released.

## Encryption
Customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Encryption keys are stored separately from the data they protect and are rotated every year.

## Incident notification
A suspected breach of customer data is reported to the privacy officer immediately. Affected customers and the relevant regulator are notified within 72 hours of confirming a breach.
