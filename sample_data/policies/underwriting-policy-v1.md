# Personal Loan Underwriting Policy (superseded)

## Scope
This earlier version of the policy applied to unsecured personal loan applications. It was replaced by version 2.0 and is kept for audit purposes only.

## Loan amounts and terms
The maximum loan amount was $35,000 and the minimum was $2,000. Approved terms were 12, 24 or 36 months.

## Credit score decision bands
A score of 660 or higher was eligible for automated approval. A score from 620 to 659 was referred to manual review. A score below 620 was declined.

## Debt-to-income limit
Total monthly debt payments, including the proposed loan payment, could not exceed 40 percent of gross monthly income.
