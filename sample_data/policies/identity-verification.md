# Customer Identity Verification Procedure

## Required information
Before an application can be decided, the applicant's full legal name, date of birth, home address and taxpayer identification number are collected and verified.

## Accepted identification
An applicant may prove identity with an unexpired government-issued photo identification, such as a driver's license, state identification card or passport. A utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 60 days is accepted as proof of address.

## Verification outcomes
If the information matches the records of the identity verification provider, the application continues automatically. If any element does not match, the application is placed on hold for manual review. An application on hold cannot be approved or funded until a reviewer approves the identity.

## Rejected applications
An identity that cannot be verified after manual review is declined, and the reason recorded is identity verification failure. The applicant may reapply with additional documents.
