# Personal Loan Underwriting Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to unsecured personal loan applications submitted by individual applicants. It does not cover mortgages, auto loans, business loans or credit cards, which are governed by separate policies.

## Loan amounts and terms
The minimum loan amount is $1,000 and the maximum loan amount is $50,000. Approved terms are 12, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months. Loans are unsecured and no collateral is taken.

## Credit score decision bands
Applications are placed in a decision band using the applicant's credit score. A score of 640 or higher is eligible for automated approval, subject to the debt-to-income limit. A score from 580 to 639 is referred to manual review by an underwriter. A score below 580 is declined, and the applicant receives an adverse action notice that states the principal reasons for the decision.

## Debt-to-income limit
The applicant's total monthly debt payments, including the proposed loan payment, may not exceed 43 percent of gross monthly income. Applications above this limit are declined unless an underwriter documents a compensating factor during manual review.

## Employment and income verification
Applicants must provide proof of income. Salaried applicants provide their two most recent pay stubs. Self-employed applicants provide their two most recent tax returns. Income that cannot be verified is excluded from the debt-to-income calculation.

## Manual review
An underwriter reviews every referred application within three business days. The underwriter records the decision, the reason and any compensating factors. A manual decision is final and cannot be overwritten by an automated rerun.

## Co-borrowers
A co-borrower may be added to strengthen an application. Both applicants are scored, and the lower credit score determines the decision band.
