# Loan Fee Schedule

## Origination fee
An origination fee of 3.0 percent of the approved loan amount is deducted from the loan proceeds at funding. The fee is disclosed in the annual percentage rate calculation and is not charged separately.

## Late payment fee
A payment is late if it is not received by the end of the grace period of 10 days after the due date. The late fee is the lesser of $35 or 5 percent of the unpaid installment. A late fee is charged at most once for each missed installment, and it is never charged on a previous late fee.

## Returned payment fee
A fee of $25 applies when a payment is returned by the borrower's bank for insufficient funds or a closed account.

## Paper statement fee
Electronic statements are free. A fee of $3 per month applies to borrowers who ask to receive paper statements.

## Prepayment
There is no prepayment penalty. Borrowers may pay a loan off early at any time without additional charges.

## Fee waivers
A servicing representative may propose a fee waiver, and a different authorized staff member must approve it. A borrower may receive one fee waiver in any 12-month period. Waivers above $100 require approval from a servicing manager.
