# Collections and Hardship Policy

## Contact rules
Collection calls may be made only between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. in the borrower's local time. Collectors may not contact a borrower at work if the borrower has said that the employer does not allow personal calls. Every contact attempt is recorded in the account notes.

## Hardship assistance
A borrower who has lost income or faces an unexpected expense may apply for hardship assistance. The borrower must explain the hardship and provide supporting documents. A decision is made within 5 business days.

## Payment deferral
An approved hardship plan may defer up to 2 monthly payments. The deferred payments are added to the end of the loan term. A borrower may receive no more than 2 deferrals in any 24-month period. Interest continues to accrue during a deferral.

## Delinquency milestones
An account is considered delinquent when a payment is more than 30 days past due. An account that is 90 days past due is referred for recovery review. A loan is charged off when it is 120 days past due.
