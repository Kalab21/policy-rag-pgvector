# Customer Complaint Handling Procedure

## Receiving a complaint
A complaint may be made by phone, email, letter or through the online portal. Any expression of dissatisfaction is treated as a complaint, whether or not the customer uses that word. Every complaint is entered in the complaint log on the day it is received.

## Acknowledgement and resolution times
The company acknowledges every complaint in writing within 2 business days. A final response is sent within 15 business days. If more time is needed, the customer is told why and given a new date, which may not be later than 35 business days from receipt.

## Escalation
A customer who is not satisfied with the response may ask for escalation. Escalated complaints are reviewed by the compliance officer, who was not involved in the original decision. Complaints that allege discrimination or unfair treatment are escalated to the compliance officer immediately.

## Records and reporting
The complaint log records the date received, the category, the outcome and the time taken. Complaint volumes and outcomes are reported to senior management every quarter.
